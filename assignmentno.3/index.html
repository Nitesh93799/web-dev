Boudhanath Stupa in Kathmandu

It is one of the largest Buddhist stupas in the world, famous for its golden dome,
all-seeing Buddha eyes, and colorful prayer flags stretching across the sky.
The stupa is an important center of Tibetan Buddhism and a symbol of peace, spirituality, and harmony. The surrounding area is lively with monasteries, shops, and pilgrims walking around the stupa in prayer.
